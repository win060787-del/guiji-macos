# Guiji Privacy Policy

Updated: October 29, 2026
Operator: NovaTech AI Limited
Contact: developer@novatechai.xyz

## Local processing
Guiji browses, reads and edits files you select. Project cards, runtime settings, credential metadata and grant digests are stored in the app’s local data directory. Tokens and passwords use app-owned macOS Keychain entries with synchronization disabled. SSH private keys are referenced by path only; their contents are not read or copied. The app has no developer-hosted backend, app accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs. Project contents, ENV values, passwords and original keys are not uploaded to the developer.

## Requests you initiate
The local grant service currently supports only reading the authenticated GitHub account. After you create a grant, start the service and invoke that operation, the app obtains the chosen key from Keychain and authenticates a request to https://api.github.com/user. Only login, id and type are returned to the caller. GitHub processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy. Guiji does not manage your GitHub account or keys and cannot recall accepted requests. Company support links, support emails and files you give other tools are your actions and subject to the recipients’ policies. The app never automatically sends project briefs to AI model services.

## Grants and exports
Temporary grant tokens are separate sensitive credentials, shown once at creation. Only their digests are retained in the app’s persistent grant records. You may copy or export them. Exported grant files contain the temporary token and must not be public or committed to public repositories. Expiry or revocation blocks future use, not previously returned data. Project/environment briefs contain configuration metadata, variable names and credential references, not original keys. You choose the destination; syncing and third-party access depend on your system and tools.

## Permissions and retention
You select files/folders for access. Notifications request permission as needed for expiry/rotation reminders. The local service is off by default, accepts only local connections and stops when the app quits. Revealing/copying keys requires your action; original credentials are not persisted as ordinary configuration text. Local records remain until deleted or app data is cleared. Removing a project card revokes its grants but preserves source files, settings and original credentials. Deleting a credential removes its metadata and requests deletion of its app-owned Keychain item; errors are reported if the system refuses. Uninstalling alone may leave Keychain items. Delete exported files and third-party copies yourself.

## Support and changes
Support emails you send may include your email address, OS version, reproduction steps and attachments, used to address the request. Do not send original keys, passwords, grant files or private keys. Contact the address above for privacy requests. This policy will be updated if processing changes, with renewed permission where necessary. The public policy URL is the one on the App Store product page.
